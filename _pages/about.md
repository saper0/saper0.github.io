I am a researcher working on topics at the intersection of machine learning and optimization. I am doing my PhD at TU Munich under the supervision of [Prof. Günnemann](https://www.in.tum.de/daml/team/guennemann/) in the [DAML](https://www.in.tum.de/daml/startseite/) research group and am part of the [relAI](https://zuseschoolrelai.de/) graduate school. My current research interests lie in ML for combinatorial optimization, in particular, using machine learning to speed up traditional mixed-integer programming solvers. More broadly, I'm also interested in neural combinatorial optimization, how predictions and optimization can be effectively combined, and in applications to societal domains such as mobility and transportation. Previously, I have worked on graph neural networks, robustness verification, and more general adversarial robustness. 

If you want to contact me, best write me an e-mail: *lukas . gosch [at] tum.de*. Scroll down to find my other social media appearances.
